One seamless ecosystem, built by every team. Each capability strengthens the rest.

Each team owns capabilities. The gateway makes them available to every product, platform and AI. Every addition makes every other capability more useful.

Assemble, don't rebuild.

A capability is a clear unit of business function: its data, its API and, where useful, a component built on the Life design system. Teams ship them on their own, and any host can put them together.

How it connects

Teams provide. One gateway serves.

Follow a request from a team's service to a person or an AI, and see who owns each step.

Domain teamsSharedAIA

1 · Teams provide

Each team opens its services.

WhatEvery team exposes what its domain can do through an API for product screens and an MCP server for AI.

WhyA user or an AI never needs to know whose backend a capability lives in.

Provided by each domain team

2 · Meaning

The same word means the same thing.

WhatA semantic layer holds shared definitions, such as what a learner or compliance rate is, written once as code. Every team's data is read through it.

WhyWithout it, each product calculates its own answer from the same records, and the numbers drift apart until no one can say which is right.

One layer, shared by every team

without  A 1,240 · B 1,187 · C 1,302
with     one definition, one number
metric   total_learners
per      program, unit

3 · Access

One door for every request.

WhatWhenever a person or an AI asks for something, the request passes through the RQI Gateway. It checks who is asking and what they may see, and logs everything.

WhyIdentity and permissions are built once, not per team. An AI never sees more than the person it works for.

Owned by AIA

4 · Output A: a component

A component any product can drop in.

WhatThe same Compliance rate component can be embedded in any product. It is built on the Life design system, so it needs no design governance of its own, and it gets its number through the gateway.

WhyA team that needs this number embeds the component instead of building its own chart.

Built by the providing team, embedded by anyone

5 · Output B: data AI can read

AI reads the data, not the screen.

WhatSamaritan AI reads the metric through MCP. The value comes back with its source, and Samaritan uses it in its answer.

WhyAI does not look at charts. It needs the number, what it means and where it came from.

Answer pattern owned by AIA

read     compliance_rate, unit 4
returns  { value: 0.87, source: "Insight" }
answer   "Compliance is 87%."

6 · Output C: AI that acts

AI can also do things.

WhatSamaritan calls a tool, such as send a reminder. The call goes through the gateway to the team that owns that service, and the result comes back.

WhyActions follow the same road as reads, so the same identity, permissions and log apply.

Tool pattern owned by AIAThe team still owns what an action does

tool call  send_reminder(unit: 4)
gateway    allowed for this admin
returns    { sent: 6 }

What it means for a team

Your product stays yours.

Stays with you

  • Your product, navigation and release schedule
  • Your roadmap and priorities
  • Ownership of what your capability does

You gain

  • Your capability reaches every host, in a product, on a platform or in an AI
  • Other teams' capabilities, ready to embed
  • Identity, permissions and logging built once, not per team

You stop

  • Recalculating the same metric as the team next door
  • Building a one-off chart for a number someone else already owns
  • Designing AI entry points alone

Your part.
